# Start here: update your website

**Doctors: open [doctor/](doctor/README.md) for your updates.** Your details, phone numbers, chamber schedule, services and professional records are all in that folder.

| Folder | Who normally edits it? | What is inside? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [doctor/](doctor/README.md) | Doctor or practice staff | Personal details, contact numbers, photograph, chamber, services, career and feedback |
| [pages/](pages/README.md) | Whoever maintains website wording | Page headings, introductions, appointment instructions, FAQ and policies |
| [settings/](settings/README.md) | Website maintainer | Website address, branding, default language and appearance |

Start with the English `en` folder. If your website also has Bengali (`bn`) or Hindi (`hi`), update the same information in that language too. Translations are not automatic. Shared phone numbers and the photograph need changing only once.

Keep field names, indentation and `---` lines unchanged. Put phone numbers in quotes. Leave `{{doctorName}}` and similar placeholders unchanged: they reuse your information automatically.

After editing, ask your website maintainer to preview, rebuild and publish. Saving a Markdown file alone does not update the live website.

বাংলা: নিয়মিত তথ্য পরিবর্তনের জন্য `doctor/` ফোল্ডার খুলুন। বাংলা তথ্য `bn/` এবং ইংরেজি তথ্য `en/` ফোল্ডারে আছে। ফোন নম্বর `doctor/contact.md` ফাইলে একবার পরিবর্তন করলেই হবে। প্রকাশের আগে ওয়েবসাইটের দায়িত্বে থাকা ব্যক্তিকে প্রিভিউ করতে বলুন।
